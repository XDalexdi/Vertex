## Hypothesis & Success Criteria

### Original Hypothesis

**We believed that:** University students with 3+ active group projects experience coordination pain severe enough that they will provide their email address to join a waitlist for a unified tool.

**We would know we were right when:** 10% or more of the users from our targeted outreach channels (specifically mentioning "3+ projects") sign up for early access.

## Experiment Execution

### What We Built/Did

We created a simple, single-page landing page on Carrd.co with the headline, "Stop Juggling 3+ Group Projects. Get One Unified Hub." The CTA was a simple email sign-up form. We posted a targeted message with the URL into 5 different university-specific student groups (Discord/Facebook) over the 7-day period.

**Deliverables:**
- [Asset 1]: TeamLearn Landing Page (Link: teamlearn.carrd.co)
- [Asset 2]: Email collection form (Airtable link)
- [Asset 3]: Distribution copy & schedule (Notion doc)

## Key Learnings

### About the Customer

1. **Learning:** The "3+ project student" segment is highly motivated and willing to try new tools.
   - **Evidence:** 12.5% conversion rate is significantly above average for this market.
   - **Implication:** Focus all marketing and feature development on this segment first.

### About the Problem

1. **Learning:** The pain of *missing deadlines* due to tool-switching chaos is a powerful motivator.
   - **Evidence:** Qualitative feedback: "I just need one place to check, so I don't panic."
   - **Implication:** The core value prop should be focused on **Coordination Confidence**.
