## 🎬 Demo Story

### The User

**User Persona:**

* **Name:** Nika
* **Background:** 2nd-year CS Student at KIU.
* **Current situation:** Overwhelmed by "Notification Blindness." He missed two Software Development labs last month simply because the emails were buried under university spam.
* **Goal:** To receive only high-priority, actionable alerts so he can submit his code on time.

**Why This Persona?**
Nika represents our core user: a high-performing student being held back by fragmented "Legacy Chaos." His success directly proves the value of our notification engine.

---

### Story Arc

**SETUP (10 seconds)**

* **What you'll say:** "Meet Nika. He’s a software development student with a 3.8 GPA, but he’s currently failing his most important module. Why? Not because he can’t code, but because he can’t find his deadlines."
* **What's on screen:** A split screen showing Nika’s cluttered Gmail, Outlook, and LMS dashboards—all overflowing with "noise."

**CHALLENGE (5 seconds)**

* **What you'll say:** "Nika just received a critical Software Development homework update via Gmail. But look—it’s already buried under 15 other notifications. He’ll never see it."
* **What's on screen:** A red circle highlighting a homework email getting pushed down by generic university newsletters.

**SOLUTION (45-60 seconds)**

* **What you'll say:** "Now, let’s look at Nika’s phone with EMS installed. Instead of 50 apps, he has one 'Clean Feed.' Watch as our engine detects that Software Development email and instantly translates it into a high-priority push notification. It doesn't just say 'You have mail'—it says 'Software Development Homework: 48 Hours Left.' With one tap, Nika is exactly where he needs to be to start coding."
* **What's on screen:**
1. Phone lock screen (Empty).
2. **The Magic Moment:** A clean, branded EMS notification pops up: *"Software Development: Lab 4 Homework assigned. Due in 48h."*
3. Nika taps it, and the app opens directly to his academic priority list.



**RESOLUTION (5-10 seconds)**

* **What you'll say:** "Nika didn't miss his deadline. He finished his homework early because he had clarity. That is the power of EMS: Turning chaos into focus."
* **What's on screen:** A green checkmark over the Software Development task and Nika looking relieved.
