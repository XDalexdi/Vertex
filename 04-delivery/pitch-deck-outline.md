## Slide 2: Problem

**The Problem:** Students face "Notification Blindness" caused by the Digital Triangle: Outlook, LMS, and Teams. 
**Quantified Impact:**

* 1-2 missed assignments per week per student. 


* Direct cause of late penalties and lower GPAs. 


* Increased student burnout due to constant "app-switching." 



---

## Slide 3: Solution

**Solution Statement:** An intelligent, student-centric bridge that connects users directly to academic priorities. 
**How It Works:**

1. 
**Centralize:** Aggregates Outlook, Teams, and LMS data into one feed. 


2. 
**Simplify:** Translates complex data into simple, actionable messages. 


3. 
**Optimize:** Delivers custom reminders when the student is most productive.
