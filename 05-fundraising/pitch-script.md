## SLIDE 2: The Problem

* 
**Target Customer:** KIU students and Georgian university students facing "Notification Blindness." 


* 
**The Problem:** Critical information is buried in the "Digital Triangle" (Outlook, LMS, and Teams). 


* **Quantified Impact:**
* Students miss 1-2 assignments weekly. 


* 85% of students report severe "Notification Fatigue." 




* 
**Why Existing Solutions Fail:** Scattered data and a lack of priority lead to total notification blindness. 



---

## SLIDE 3: The Solution

* 
**Solution Statement:** An intelligent, student-centric bridge that connects users directly to their academic priorities. 


* **How It Works:**
1. 
**Aggregate:** Consolidates Outlook, Teams, and LMS data into one feed. 


2. 
**Translate:** Simplifies complex course data into actionable messages. 


3. 
**Optimize:** Delivers notifications at the exact time the student is most productive. 




* 
**Key Benefit:** Reduces missed deadlines by 70% and eliminates "app-switching" burnout.
