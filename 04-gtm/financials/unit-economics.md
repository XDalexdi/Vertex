LIFETIME VALUE (LTV)
LTV Calculation
text
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin %) × Customer Lifetime
Revenue Metrics
Metric	Value	Source/Validation
ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)	$8.33/month	Premium subscription ($100/year)
Gross Margin %	88%	Low COGS (mostly infrastructure)
Monthly Churn Rate	3%	From interviews: strong retention due to project lock-in
Customer Lifetime	33.3 months	1 / 3% monthly churn
LTV Calculation
text
LTV = ($8.33 × 88%) × 33.3 months
LTV = $7.33 × 33.3
LTV = $244
Annual LTV: $88 ($7.33 × 12 months)
