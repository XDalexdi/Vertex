# Unit Economics Analysis
**Team:** Vertex  
**Product:** TeamLearn  
**Date:** December 23, 2025  
**Analysis Period:** First 12 months post-MVP launch

---

## **Executive Summary**

Based on validated metrics from Experiment 1 (12.5% conversion) and detailed customer interviews, TeamLearn demonstrates **exceptionally strong unit economics** with an **LTV:CAC ratio of 3.1:1** and **payback period of 10.8 months**. These metrics indicate highly capital-efficient growth potential in our target segment (students with 3+ group projects).

### **Revenue Metrics**

| Time Period | Expected Churn | Retention Strategy |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|

## **Unit Economics Ratios**

### **Primary Ratios**

| Ratio | Formula | Calculation | Result | Target | Status |
|-------|---------|-------------|--------|--------|--------|
| **LTV:CAC** | LTV ÷ CAC | $244 ÷ $79 | **3.1:1** | >3:1 | ✅ **Healthy** |
| **Payback Period** | CAC ÷ (ARPU × GM%) | $79 ÷ $7.33 | **10.8 months** | <12 months | ✅ **Healthy** |
| **Months to Recover CAC** | CAC ÷ Monthly Contribution | $79 ÷ $7.33 | **10.8 months** | <12 months | ✅ **Healthy** |

### **Secondary Efficiency Metrics**

| Metric | Calculation | Result | Benchmark |
|--------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| **Magic Number** | (Revenue(t) - Revenue(t-1)) × 4 ÷ S&M Spend(t-1) | 1.8 | >0.75 = Efficient |
| **CAC Ratio** | CAC ÷ ARPU | 9.5 | <12 = Good |
| **Gross Margin Adjusted LTV:CAC** | (LTV × GM%) ÷ CAC | 2.7:1 | >2:1 = Good |

## **Appendix: Calculation Details**

### **Detailed CAC Calculation**
Monthly S&M Spend = $2,100 (average Months 4-12)
New Paying Customers = 28/month
CAC = $2,100 ÷ 28 = $75

Including free users (80% conversion):
New Total Users = 35/month
Blended CAC = $2,100 ÷ 35 = $60

### **Detailed LTV Calculation**
Monthly ARPU = $8.33
Gross Margin = 88%
Monthly Contribution = $8.33 × 88% = $7.33
Monthly Churn = 3%
Customer Lifetime = 1 ÷ 0.03 = 33.3 months
LTV = $7.33 × 33.3 = $244

text

### **Payback Period**
Monthly Contribution = $7.33
CAC = $79
Payback = $79 ÷ $7.33 = 10.8 months
